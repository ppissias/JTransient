## Alternatives that remain research

No universal correlation or three-bin veto, wider default axis ratio, automatic maximum-stack deduplication, residual-fit replacement, covariance-confidence gate, star-mask/clipping removal or lower default score is adopted from these experiments. Gains/losses, controls, unavailable cases and counterexamples are retained in the study folders.

The completed investigation round retains current filters/defaults and adds no further production science change. Earlier temporal-bin injection gains remain conditional; the native follow-up adds no detection. Unresolved scientific limits do not keep the whole campaign open: only the deferred [SM-F7 extension item](../../../FEATURE-REQUESTS.md#sm-f7-slow-and-faint-mover-extensions) remains for concrete missed-source or resource needs. Existing study snapshots retain their historical conclusions.
