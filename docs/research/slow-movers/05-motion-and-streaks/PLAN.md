## Native streak audit

Use the existing wider-shape candidates in Klangwolke-1 and Apophis as development examples, not independent validation. Read every previously QC-retained original-frame crop, reproduce the saved native maximum/median crops exactly, attribute each bright residual pixel to its original maximum-contributing frame, and inspect every frame. The review region is the measured oriented candidate bounding rectangle and residual >= five times the median native-frame crop noise. It is explicitly not the exact detector footprint. Compare independently saved production streak footprints, not just their centroids: a long streak can overlap a candidate far from its centroid. Retain multiple pieces in a frame; they are not conflicting identities for an overlap diagnostic. Record per-frame contact with supported aperture centres, dominant source frames and when the aperture is instead supported away from a streak. This is provenance, not automatic rejection.

## Frozen review hypothesis

Original-frame aperture positions are borrowed unchanged from SlowMoverFrameEvidence. Compute relative exposure-midpoint times from original frame identities, requiring finite increasing capture times and positive exposure lengths. Never substitute frame ordinal for elapsed time. Use only the supported time span, without extrapolating through unsupported ends. Split supported positions into three equal-time bins and require at least two positions per bin, at least six overall and at least four training points per fold. Fit a straight and a quadratic path separately in X/Y, using normalized time, and leave each contiguous time bin out once. Record each model's held-out RMS and the lower of the two; this is an empirical model check, not a significance or identity probability. Missing bins/rank/clock evidence stays unavailable.

Freeze the review tolerance at max(2px,2*existing maxStarJitter), not a new engine setting. Call a path temporally compatible when at least one model's held-out RMS is within that tolerance and its fitted positions across the supported span separate by at least 2px. A larger mismatch means the tested models lack support, not proof of a false detection. Independently map the same positions to full-frame camera coordinates using fill-derived FrameRegistration, with tile origins restored. Require usable registrations on >=80% of positions; report RMS radius around the median camera position and registration fit residuals. Camera compatibility uses max(1px,existing maxStarJitter) plus the median registration-fit RMS in quadrature as an explicitly empirical review tolerance. A path compatible in both systems is ambiguous; steady registration drift can make the same pixel history consistent with a real mover or a camera artifact. Do not suppress such findings.

The study measures a hypothetical strict motion-compatible counter and a hypothetical temporal-mismatch rejection counter. Neither enters production. Count losses of known/associated movers explicitly and retain timing/camera-unavailable cases separately. Do not tune any threshold after viewing held-out outcomes. Centroid uncertainties, blended-source selection, systematic registration errors and exposure-integrated flux remain limitations; normalized ratios are not astrometric error bars.

Local streak contact is measured independently of truth by re-extracting a bounded candidate-centred crop (48-128px) from each supported native frame, with the existing per-frame thresholds and streak morphology. Contact means a detected streak pixel is within the candidate aperture radius plus one pixel of the supported centroid. This local re-extraction can differ from full-field extraction, so it is a review diagnostic, not a production streak mask. Also compute a secondary trajectory review after omitting those contact positions; do not choose between reviews based on recovery or use that secondary result as the primary frozen counter.

## Fresh copied-pixel controls

Use all 18 reference datasets, with the original five development sessions reported separately from 13 held-out datasets. These are fresh source locations/seeds on the existing observations, not independent new nights or sky fields. Freeze two 256px tiles at full-field fractions (0.37,0.27) and (0.71,0.61); retain every frame in the accepted QC manifests. Read full-frame registration facts once per image before copying tiles. Gaussian sigma1.2px and Moffat FWHM4.4px/beta3.5 sources, nominal aperture-amplitude SNR10/20, 20% sinusoidal seeing variation at approximately fixed total flux. Subpixel centre (127.41,128.17). Seven equal-weight sub-exposure samples use actual durations for moving sources. No unknown source gain/photon-noise calibration is invented.

Per background/profile/amplitude: nine straight paths (8/16/24px, directions -1.3/0.2/1.7); four curved paths (12/20px, bows3/6px); two gapped paths (12/20px, direction0.2, omit every fourth injection and phase0.43-0.57); two blends (16px path crosses a stationary source three/ten times the moving flux); four moving-plus-streak cases (16px path, one/two contaminated frames, streak directions0.2/1.0). A synthetic streak has length70px, Gaussian width1.3px and peak30 times the first tile noise. Bright streaks exist only in declared original frames. Truth is reserved for recovery matching; motion diagnostics never read it.

Controls per background/profile/amplitude: four independently shuffled source paths (12/20px x two new fixed permutation seeds), one stationary source, one stationary source with changing width, two stationary-source-plus-streak cases, and one camera-or-drift-degenerate case. The latter is one image history with two possible physical interpretations, not two independent samples: source centres follow a fixed camera position under the measured registrations. It must not be labelled a confirmed physical mover or artifact from these coordinates alone. Add one unchanged background per tile.
