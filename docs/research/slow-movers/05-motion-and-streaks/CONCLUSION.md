# Motion/streak follow-up: completed

Historical investigation snapshot. Statements about implementation, pending work or publication describe the experiment at its date. Current behavior is documented in [ALGORITHM.md](../../../../ALGORITHM.md); current decisions remain in [FEATURE-REQUESTS.md](../../../../FEATURE-REQUESTS.md). Local artifact names in the snapshot are not promises that every external cache or harness is bundled. See this study's [inventory](README.md).

The visible bright streaks were not intended faint-mover injections. They are actual streak detections in the native sequences. In the original expanded-shape trial, the maximum-stack slow-mover stage also accepted contaminated footprints. Its acceptance did not explain or veto the contamination, even though ordinary streak tracks already existed elsewhere in the result.

The frame-by-frame audit reconstructs saved maximum/median crops exactly. All 147 bright review-region pixels in Klangwolke-1 come from original frame2; all92 in Apophis come from frame5. Production raw streak footprints intersect the supported aperture position in those exposures. Other exposures can contain nearby trails, especially Apophis. The review region is an oriented measured rectangle with max-minus-median >=5 native-noise units, not the exact detector footprint. The proof figures show every retained frame and preserve original numbering.

Proceeding with the authorised next motion study: freeze a straight/quadratic three-time-bin held-out prediction check at exposure midpoints plus a separate camera-coordinate radius check. Tolerances are empirical review scales, not astrometric uncertainty or calibrated significance. Missing/ambiguous evidence remains explicit. Independently detected local streak contact is secondary provenance; no whole-finding mask or veto is used.

All4,356 predeclared cases across18 datasets are accounted for:4,086 evaluated and270 excluded. Whynot lacks usable exposure lengths for midpoint testing (242 cases);28 camera-path cases leave the fixed crops. No source position was moved and no saturation silently clipped. Five development datasets and13 held-out datasets remain separate; new source locations/seeds use existing observations, not independent new nights.

On held-out moving cases, maximum-stack association recovers674/2,016. A hypothetical temporal-mismatch veto retains642, losing32 (six straight, eleven curved, fifteen gapped). Strict motion-compatible review retains586. Associated shuffled survivors fall239->23 with the hypothetical veto, or2 under strict review; these are controls, not a survey false-positive rate. None of192 evaluated blends and only1/384 mover-plus-streak cases reaches the association rule before confirmation, so this trial cannot establish safe recovery through those overlaps. Do not adopt the tested veto or a blanket streak rejection.

Native review remains cautious: Valeria is temporally compatible; the Eagle extra fails the tested models; K1/Apophis have insufficient bin coverage; whynot has camera-compatible geometry but no usable exposure-midpoint review. These are review outcomes, not physical classifications. The camera/steady-sky-drift degeneracy remains real and is verified by scientific boundary controls.

No production source, threshold, default, setting, report or finding is changed. Original818 image SHA256 hashes and frozen harness/profile/library hashes are verified separately in validation.json. Numeric boundary controls pass; resource replays reproduce exact arrays and scientific JSON values. Only the three existing tracked documentation files are updated, with no branch/commit/push. No production unit/UI suites are rerun for documentation-only changes.

Next prerequisites in SM-F3 are better candidate/source-centroid evidence and uncertainty, explicit per-frame streak-footprint provenance and preservation of real movers crossing trails. A long streak's centroid can be far from the overlap; use its raw footprint and preserve multiple pieces/frame for provenance rather than treating them as a point-track identity. Do not tune a new gate on these outcomes and call the same cases an independent validation.
