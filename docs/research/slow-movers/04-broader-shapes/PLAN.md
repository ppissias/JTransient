# SM-F3 held-out broader-shape validation

Historical investigation snapshot. Statements about implementation, pending work or publication describe the experiment at its date. Current behavior is documented in [ALGORITHM.md](../../../../ALGORITHM.md); current decisions remain in [FEATURE-REQUESTS.md](../../../../FEATURE-REQUESTS.md). Local artifact names in the snapshot are not promises that every external cache or harness is bundled. See this study's [inventory](README.md).

Frozen before execution, 2026-11-04. Authorized scope: validate the existing raw maximum-stack route with geometric maximum axis ratio 2.8 versus 5. All other thresholds, frame quality rules and evidence gates stay fixed. No production science/default changes, automatic duplicate removal, temporal veto, residual route, commit or push are part of this experiment.

The five development sessions are Klangwolke-2, Eagle-noisy, M81 Kumar, helix-reg and m101-reg. The other 13 reference datasets are held out from the prior SM-F3 studies (not independent observations of every astronomical field: M31 bands share a target). All 18 full-size sessions are rerun once with the frozen shipped profile and normal photometry selection. Baseline science and stack hashes must reproduce the latest accepted outputs, ignoring only measured photometry processing time and faint-search elapsed time. No new reports are exported.

Re-run both shape limits on the same resident maximum/median stacks and retained original frames, with the full production data area. Every baseline candidate, mask and its evidence must be preserved; only the explicit upper-shape threshold in diagnostics may differ. Record every additional candidate, full original-frame centroid evidence and common-frame comparisons to ALL plausible ordinary tracks. Footprint proximity is not an identity decision. Also record compared-frame coverage, supported time coverage, RMS, maximum discrepancy, ambiguity and conflicting timestamps. Do not suppress or call a candidate new solely because it has no track match.

For each of the 13 held-out sessions, copy two 256-pixel native tiles centred at field fractions (0.29,0.37) and (0.63,0.71), independent of source locations. Use every QC-retained frame and the actual cadence; retain all original frame IDs, gaps and backgrounds. Injections modify copied arrays only. Calibrate nominal peak amplitude to the first tile frame's clipped noise and a radius-2.5px aperture; this is an amplitude definition, not measured SNR or calibrated completeness. No source photon-noise/gain model is added. Subpixel centre is (128.23,128.61). Source profiles are Gaussian sigma 1.1px and Moffat FWHM 4px/beta 3.5, with sinusoidal 15% seeing variation at approximately constant total flux. Nominal amplitude-SNR is 7.5 or 15. Saturation aborts a case; it must never silently clip.

282 predeclared cases per held-out session (3,666 total):

- 96 straight movers: two backgrounds x two profiles x two amplitudes x endpoint displacement 6/12/20/28px x direction -0.7/0.8/2.5 radians.
- 32 curved movers: backgrounds/profiles/amplitudes x displacement 12/20px x midpoint bow 3/6px, direction 0.8.
- 32 gapped movers: backgrounds/profiles/amplitudes x displacement 12/20px x direction -0.7/0.8. Omit injection on every fifth retained frame and at phase 0.42-0.58 without deleting original frames.
- 96 shuffled-position controls: backgrounds/profiles/amplitudes x displacement 12/20px x direction -0.7/0.8 x three fixed new permutation seeds. Only injected positions are permuted; native pixels and timestamps are unchanged.
- 8 stationary sources, 8 stationary sources with 30% seeing changes, 8 single-frame flashes (five times base peak), and 2 unchanged background tiles.

Associate a candidate to truth with at least max(3,ceil(0.4*N)) active-frame positions, active-frame RMS <=2px and at least 80% of its supported positions in active frames. For gapped sources N is the full retained frame count. These labels measure injection-associated recovery; record all candidate counts separately. Uninjected tiles are not certified empty. Flash association cannot satisfy three active frames, so also report spatially nearby candidates. Run both shape configurations explicitly, alternating execution order; assert exact baseline-candidate inclusion with unchanged geometry and evidence. Never reject a case because of unavailable timing or correlation. No threshold is tuned after inspecting results.

Measure elapsed stage time and peak process memory. Count uncertainty is descriptive: use session-stratified paired recovery differences and deterministic session bootstrap, with grouping by astrophotography target for M31 bands; do not treat every injected case as independent sky or infer a survey false-positive rate. Compare null-control load on matching footprints and record every exclusion/failure. Rehash the original 818-image manifest at completion and verify frozen profile/library/source hashes. Review added full-field candidates against originals and measured track agreement before making a recommendation. If default changes remain unsupported, document the experiment and leave production behavior unchanged.

Harness correction before accepted execution: the first K1 attempt failed its case-count guard at 234 rather than 282 because the shuffled-control direction loop was missing. That incomplete attempt and its original manifest/plan are preserved under pilot-count-check. The two shuffled directions are now explicit above and implemented. No detection threshold, positive example, association threshold, source profile or case exclusion was changed after this pilot; its recovery outputs were not summarized for rule selection. The accepted run repeats K1 from scratch and requires all 282 cases per held-out dataset.
