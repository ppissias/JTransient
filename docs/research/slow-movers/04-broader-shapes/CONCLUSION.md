# SM-F3 broader-shape validation: completed

Historical investigation snapshot. Statements about implementation, pending work or publication describe the experiment at its date. Current behavior is documented in [ALGORITHM.md](../../../../ALGORITHM.md); current decisions remain in [FEATURE-REQUESTS.md](../../../../FEATURE-REQUESTS.md). Local artifact names in the snapshot are not promises that every external cache or harness is bundled. See this study's [inventory](README.md).

Recommendation: retain the existing default maximum axis ratio of 2.8. The existing control already permits a sensitivity experiment at 5, but this study does not support promoting it to the default or adding automatic duplicate suppression.

The frozen comparison completed 3,666 paired cases across the 13 reference datasets outside the prior five-session development set. All 2,080 moving cases and 1,586 controls were evaluated without exclusions. Moving recovery increased from 371 (17.8%) to 670 (32.2%); shuffled-position survivors increased from 201/1,248 (16.1%) to 554/1,248 (44.4%). Both PSF templates and nominal amplitudes show recovery gains. The selected-grid target-cluster percentile interval for the paired recovery increase is 8.6–19.0 percentage points; M31 bands share one cluster. This is not survey completeness, significance or a false-positive-rate calibration.

Across 18 complete native fields, 36 baseline morphology candidates become 42. All baseline candidates, geometry, median masks and scalar evidence are preserved exactly (apart from the deliberately different upper-shape threshold). The six additions are:

- Klangwolke-2: a footprint compatible with Valeria's already existing ordinary track; 27 common frames, 0.1456px RMS, 0.3021px maximum separation, no ambiguous IDs/times.
- Klangwolke-1 and Apophis: native frames show trail contamination inside the wider footprint. K1 has a recorded long capture gap and only a spatial, ambiguous-frame streak comparison. Neither is an established new mover.
- whynot: two camera-fixed-compatible artifacts. Their registered/camera RMS radii are 9.563/0.289px and 8.914/0.488px across 59 frames; both have absolute correlation above .999. Steady drift remains degenerate with some sky paths, so these are diagnostic comparisons rather than an adopted veto.
- Eagle-noisy: a patchy footprint with no confirmed moving identity; its supported centroids do not establish an ordered path.

The camera check and earlier curved-mover counterexample argue against adopting a correlation-only rejection threshold. The next scientific work should validate candidate-local temporal and camera-coordinate evidence, centroid uncertainty, exposure timing, coverage, blends and competing-track associations on fresh held-out cases. Do not tune on these validation outcomes and reuse them as an independent test.

All 18 accepted scientific snapshots and maximum/median/veto-mask hashes reproduce exactly. All 818 original image hashes remain unchanged. Frozen settings/libraries/harness hashes are unchanged. Only three tracked documentation files changed; production sources, settings, reports and branches remain unchanged. No commit or push was made. No unit/UI suites were rerun for these documentation-only changes; paired execution, baseline reproduction, input/artifact integrity, 213 local documentation links/anchors and whitespace checks provide the relevant verification.

Evidence is in RESULTS.md, summary.json, case-summary.json, dataset-summary.csv, validation-summary.png/svg, six native candidate inspection figures, camera-review.json, per-dataset paired outputs and validation.json. The largest observed full-study process working set is 8.78GiB. Stage timings are single paired observations rather than a controlled performance benchmark.
