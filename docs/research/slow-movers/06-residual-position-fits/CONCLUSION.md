# Candidate-local position study: conclusion

Historical investigation snapshot. Statements about implementation, pending work or publication describe the experiment at its date. Current behavior is documented in [ALGORITHM.md](../../../../ALGORITHM.md); current decisions remain in [FEATURE-REQUESTS.md](../../../../FEATURE-REQUESTS.md). Local artifact names in the snapshot are not promises that every external cache or harness is bundled. See this study's [inventory](README.md).

The alternative is useful evidence for blended sources, not a validated replacement for current frame measurements or a motion-confidence gate. Production engine code, defaults, settings and candidate acceptance remain unchanged.

The frozen experiment declares2,052 cases on18 existing observations;1,910 evaluate. Whynot's114 midpoint-based cases lack exposure lengths, and28 fixed camera paths leave their crops. All copied tiles have zero fill pixels. The original five development sessions and other13 are separate comparison cohorts; neither is a new independent observing campaign. Current raw gates reproduce exactly for every reviewed proposal.

These are pre-frame-gate measurement histories, not final detection recoveries. Eighteen of213 already accepted, associated cases no longer have an associated fitted history. Lost histories include low/no positive residual peaks and singular/unconverged/displaced or low-SNR fits; no research footprint-work bound accounts for those losses. Shuffled-position histories105->110/192 remain a temporal failure mode, not a calibrated false-positive rate.

All192 other-cohort mover-plus-trail cases yield no associated history in either measurement on qualified proposals. Across both cohorts, components touching>=40% of those injected paths fail shape/fill qualification before the measurement. Therefore zero reported streak contacts in those source proposals does not establish uncontaminated sources or safe trail-crossing recovery. The earlier native streak provenance proof remains applicable; blanket contact vetoing is not justified.

Source lock-on improves in the blend controls, but evidence availability differs. Across truth-covering geometry-qualified moving proposals, raw/fitted active positions10219/8497 and stationary-source locks3412/444. Covariance algebra passes the correctly specified Gaussian/noise control381/399 inside a nominal95% ellipse. On native-background injected histories it covers only2880/8497(33.9%) other-cohort positions and2000/5650(35.4%) development positions, without selecting positions by measured error. The fixed PSF, shared median, correlated/native noise, source confusion and selection invalidate treating this conditional precision as calibrated astrometry.

The exact cached Prokne footprint replays all28 raw centroids within1.51e-13px. Residual fitting also supplies28 positions. Against12 common ordinary-track measurements, fitted RMS/max is.08816/.17756px versus the previous raw agreement of about.077/.116px. This comparison is not truth or a full astrometric error model. Median formal X sigma~.001765px and fit-residual/noise ratio~18.05 reinforce the model limitation. The other expanded-shape native raw footprints were not cached, so no invented replacement is described as an exact replay.

Keep current raw evidence and default2.8. Further work should separate moving and stationary sources with a credible PSF/noise/availability model and address candidate construction through trails. An additive diagnostic can be investigated while retaining raw support; choosing between methods by truth here would be an oracle, not an implementable validated rule. Do not tune a new threshold on these outcomes and call the same examples independent validation. P1/P2/P3 remain open. SM-F2/F4/F5 are unchanged.
