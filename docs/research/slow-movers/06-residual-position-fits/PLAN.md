## One alternative measurement

Keep the raw maximum-stack component construction, size/shape/fill and median-overlap definitions unchanged, with the existing manual axis limit 5 used for a fixed proposal pool. Record the original production acceptance and first failed gate. Within size/shape/fill-qualified components touching a fixed central 96-pixel-square review region, compare current raw-frame strongest-aperture positions with one alternative: subtract the same sequence temporal median, estimate a local signed-residual annulus median/MAD, select the highest fixed Gaussian matched response at the component's raw-pixel locations, then fit a Gaussian of sigma 1.5 pixels plus a constant background in a 9x9 native-pixel window. Fit amplitude and X/Y, constrain the solution to within two pixels of its initial maximum, and require fitted amplitude SNR >=5 for a reported alternative position. No trajectory/truth/catalogue is used by measurement. Research work is bounded to 2,048 footprint pixels; larger cases are explicitly unavailable rather than sampled or relocated.

The 4-parameter least-squares normal matrix gives a formal X/Y covariance conditional on the fixed PSF and locally measured independent constant noise. Preserve singular/edge/nonpositive/unconverged/low-SNR states. Record fit residual/noise ratio without rejecting on it. This covariance ignores shared-median/correlated noise, PSF mismatch, source photon noise and selection effects; measure its actual injection coverage and never call it calibrated astrometric uncertainty. A scalar synthetic Gaussian/noise control checks fit/covariance algebra before native runs.

Extract streak components once per copied native frame with the existing ordinary extractor settings. Record the distance to actual detected streak pixels for raw/alternative positions, preserving multiple pieces and original frame identities. These tile-local extractions are provenance diagnostics, not the full-frame production streak masks or candidate vetoes.

## Fresh cases and fixed domain

Read new 256-pixel tiles at field fractions (0.43,0.69) and (0.77,0.33) from all 18 existing reference observations, preserving QC-retained identities, timestamps, exposure lengths and fill registrations. Report the five original development datasets separately from the other 13; these are existing observing nights. The fixed review region is [80,176) in both coordinates and is identical for controls. Source centre (129.23,125.81). Gaussian sigma1.3 or Moffat FWHM4.8/beta3.5; nominal aperture-amplitude SNR10/20; 20% sinusoidal seeing at approximately constant flux. Seven actual-exposure sub-samples for motion; missing/nonchronological midpoint times, out-of-crop camera paths and saturation are explicit exclusions.

For each tile/profile/amplitude: straight displacement8/16/24px at angle0.43; curved16px with bow5px; gapped20px omitting every fifth injection and phase0.41-0.57; crossing blends at stationary/moving peak ratios3/10; moving plus one/two original-frame 70px bright trails (angle1.1,width1.3,peak30 native noise); shuffled16px paths using two new fixed seeds; stationary fixed/changing width; camera-fixed/drift ambiguity history. Fourteen configurations x two profiles x two amplitudes x two tiles plus two blank tiles =114 cases/dataset, 2,052 declared. Do not move sources or tune a threshold after results.

## Measurements and acceptance

Verify the independently audited raw gates reproduce production candidate acceptance for every measured proposal. Truth is used only afterwards: original association requires >=max(3,ceil(0.4*N)) active positions, >=80% of supported positions active, RMS<=2px. Record raw versus residual-fit associated histories before/after the unchanged median gate, loss/gain pairs, common-frame errors, fit availability, stationary-source lock-on in blends, local streak contact and formal-covariance coverage. Record candidate-construction/shape/mask losses separately; an improved position estimator cannot recover absent/rejected proposals by itself. Preserve all background candidates in counts rather than declaring blank controls empty. Native known Prokne/Valeria and K1/Apophis footprints receive a separate diagnostic replay where their saved component can be reproduced; no physical identity is inferred for the others.

Freeze source/config/library/plan hashes before accepted execution. Inspect paired plots and representative failures, verify original image hashes and frozen inputs, and publish only numerical findings/limitations to the existing ALGORITHM/SM-F3 documentation. No universal rejection rule, broader default, replacement evidence route, completeness or false-positive calibration follows from these selected grids. A production science change remains a separate decision.
