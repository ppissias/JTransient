## Frozen domain and cases

Use all 18 reference observations and their previously accepted QC-retained frame identities, UTC captures/exposures and native signed-short input samples. Read two new, fixed 256px tiles centred at field fractions (.18,.58) and (.68,.21), without relocating unsuitable tiles. Record registration-fill counts and exclude a tile from this isolated gate experiment if it contains fill; do not interpret that as validation of coverage/fill rules. Existing full-frame metadata/manifests supply identities only. Original inputs are read-only. Cache/source/library/profile hashes are retained.

Every tile receives a blank control and 14 configurations for each Gaussian/Moffat profile and nominal aperture-amplitude SNR 6/14. Source centre (117.63,137.29), direction .67rad; Gaussian sigma1.25 or Moffat FWHM4.4/beta3.5; sinusoidal width changes of 20% at approximately fixed flux. Integrate seven samples over actual exposures. Add no photon noise, since native detector gain is not established; these are paired artificial histories on real backgrounds, not physical completeness estimates.

The configurations are: straight displacement4/12/24px; curved16px/bow4px; gapped16px (omit every sixth sample and phase .31-.55); sparse16px (only retained indices congruent1 modulo4 illuminated); blend16px with a stationary source five times brighter; moving16px crossing two 70px trails (angle1.27,width1.3,peak30 native-noise units) at one/three and two/three of the sequence; one shuffled16px history with a new fixed seed; stationary round; stationary elongated by2.2 along the fixed axis; one-frame elongated flash; straight12px with capture clocks deliberately withheld only after pixel generation; camera-fixed source using the native registrations. Missing native exposure/timing, nonchronological midpoints, saturation, out-of-crop camera paths and contaminated tiles are explicit exclusions, without replacing cases. ## SM-F1 prototype

Build three equal-elapsed-time groups from valid exposure midpoints, each with at least two frames. For each bin compute a native lower-median stack, subtract the full sequence median and apply the existing fixed-sigma1.5 signed-residual Gaussian fit inside the same component footprint. Choose no source by truth, catalogue or trajectory. Missing clocks/groups/fits and low-SNR results remain explicit. Record three fitted positions and residual/noise metrics; formal covariance is conditional and is not calibrated uncertainty.

One frozen empirical review calls three available positions compatible when endpoint displacement is at least max(2px,2*jitter), the middle position lies within that tolerance of time-linear endpoint interpolation, the two segments do not reverse (nonnegative dot product), and endpoint direction is within45deg of the footprint major axis (unsigned). This is a scratch diagnostic, not confirmation. It intentionally exposes curved/gapped failures. No exact displacement/footprint-extent identity is required.

Compare baseline plus an available-incompatible veto (unavailable retained), and the same veto after removing both median upper and stationary ceiling. Also record a strict bin-compatible substitution for that pair, with unavailable separated. Report the incremental losses/control survivors and computation; neither hypothetical policy is adopted.

## Independent scoring and checks

Primary raw-history association requires at least3 supported positions on illuminated original frames, at least80% of all reported positions in those active frames, and RMS distance to injected positions at most2px. There is no fraction-of-retained support requirement and no bin-coverage condition: the tested40% floor is not built into success. Report support/time coverage, individual-position errors and source-lock behavior separately. Bin-position scoring compares to the mean active injected positions in each actual bin; this is a declared reference for a moving stack, not exact astrometric truth. Report raw and bin associations separately, never selecting whichever happens to fit truth.

Count each case's associated accepted histories, all accepted ROI components and unassessed components under each variant. Stationary, shuffled, camera and flash controls remain separately labelled; blank tiles can contain real native sources, so their candidates are unverified load rather than certified false positives. Report paired gains/losses, native background counts, missing evidence and time/memory observations by cohort and mode, with per-dataset results. Do not tune on these outcomes and relabel them held-out validation.

Before native-case execution, compile and pass mathematical fit, association-independence, paired-gate and temporal-geometry controls; freeze the plan, scripts, sources, profile, jars, compiled classes and input manifests. Hash copied tile data immediately after preparation, before evaluating its injected cases. Afterwards inspect plots and representative failures, verify frozen artifacts/source/input hashes and Markdown/privacy checks, and record findings in the existing algorithm/backlog. Any production recommendation must be supported by the combined evidence and later fresh validation; keeping all scientific gates is a valid outcome.
