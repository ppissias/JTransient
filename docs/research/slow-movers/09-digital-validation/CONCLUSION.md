Camera controls need particular caution: four actual camera trajectories cannot fit a straight path within one search FWHM; the two more linear ones drift below the nominal search speed. Thus zero camera-source associations are not a clean independent test of camera rejection. The code/predicate audit proves camera share implies the margin for finite scores>=4, explaining the margin-only policy's inactivity at8/6. Positive score settings below4 remain valid and can distinguish the predicates (score3,camera1.25). Do not remove their general behavior. Quiet footprints also leave streak/duplicate and some other gates insufficiently tested. Local-background clipping, upstream QC and input validity were not separately ablated.

Star-crossing/source separation, proposal/refinement losses, eligible steady camera drift, close/parallel/trail paths, short sequences, uncertainty and independent observations remain decision priorities.
