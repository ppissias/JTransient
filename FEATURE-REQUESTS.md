# Feature requests and algorithm proposals

Proposed changes to what JTransient detects, measures or tunes. How the code is organised is in [DESIGN-IMPROVEMENTS.md](DESIGN-IMPROVEMENTS.md), and application features in SpacePixels' [feature requests](../SpacePixels/FEATURE-REQUESTS.md).

These are not authorisations. Keep the existing algorithms until the stated evidence is available and the maintainer decides. An algorithm change goes one at a time, with recovery, false positives and run time measured on the reference datasets (see [DEVELOPER.md](DEVELOPER.md#real-data-checks)). When a proposal is implemented, describe it in [ALGORITHM.md](ALGORITHM.md) and remove it from this file.

## ST-6. Faint-streak search

Requested by the maintainer on 2026-11-01 for SpacePixels [F17](../SpacePixels/FEATURE-REQUESTS.md#f17-faint-streak-search): a second, streak-only extraction on smoothed frames that adds faint streaks and streak tracks to the normal results, enabled by default. Oriented line filters are not pursued at this stage.

**Why.** A trail whose pixels are 1–1.5σ above the sky never gives a 3.5σ seed or a connected blob, although the eye sees it along its length. Smoothing each frame with a Gaussian the size of the stars (a matched filter) lowers the pixel noise more than the trail, and the existing extraction then finds it. A non-linear stretch such as asinh does not help: the extraction measures the noise again on the stretched frame, and asinh compresses everything above the sky relative to that noise.

**Evidence** (scratch experiment, 2026-11-01): filtered copies of the frames through the unchanged pipeline with the default profile.

- Integral-spacecraft-challenge has a trail in frames 1–10, 0.7–1.5σ per pixel at its ridge. It is not found in the original frames, nor after asinh at three strengths. With Gaussian smoothing of σ = 1.25 px (star FWHM 2.9 px) it becomes a streak track over frames 4, 5 and 7–10, with pieces in frame 1 rescued as anomalies on its path.
- With σ = 1 px, the streak results of Eagle-noisy, whynot and klangwolke-2 gained no false streak. klangwolke-2 lost one short single-frame streak and gained one real faint one.
- Running the whole pipeline on smoothed frames is not usable: point findings multiplied (whynot: moving targets 0 → 716, camera-fixed groups 2 → 583, run time 10 s → 15 min), and quality control rejected two Integral frames that the normal run keeps. Hence a streak-only pass inside the normal run.

**Proposal.**

1. **The normal pass is unchanged.** Extraction, quality control, data area, median master and master stars run as today. The faint pass uses the normal pass's kept frames, data area and registrations; it does not run quality control again.
2. **Smoothing.** A Gaussian with σ = session star size / 2.355. The session star size is the median, over the kept frames with a genuine measurement, of `medianFWHM` divided by √2, because the extraction FWHM is about √2 above a Gaussian FWHM ([EX-3](#declined)); without any measurement σ is 1.0 px (review finding 1). Registration fill stays as it is and is not smoothed into the image (a normalised convolution over image pixels only).
3. **Faint extraction.** Each kept frame is smoothed and extracted with the run's thresholds; the sky and noise are measured on the smoothed frame. Each worker handles one frame at a time and keeps only the detections, so no smoothed frame outlives its extraction.
   - Streak-shaped objects (`streakMinElongation`, `streakMinPixels`) are faint streak candidates.
   - Other objects are kept only as possible pieces of a streak's path (step 6). They never become findings themselves.
4. **Faint star mask.** The median master is smoothed the same way and its stars extracted with the master settings. Faint candidates are vetoed against this mask (`maxMaskOverlapFraction`) and by the binary-star shape check, because smoothing reveals fainter stars and joins close pairs into elongated blobs.
5. **Combination with the normal streaks (normal priority; review findings 2, 6 and 7).** A faint candidate that duplicates a normal streak detection of the same frame (shared pixels and the same angle) is dropped, and the normal measurement is kept. Normal streak linking runs first, exactly as today. The remaining faint candidates are linked among themselves and can then only join existing tracks through the existing consolidation, or form new tracks. A faint detection can therefore extend a normal streak track into more frames, join two pieces of one trail, or form a new streak track or single-frame streak; a normal streak detection is never moved out of its track. Normal anomalies on a faint streak's path join it, as they join any streak today. Faint objects that end in no streak track are discarded; they never reach anomaly rescue or the unclassified exports.
6. **Path pieces (proposed for deferral; review finding 7).** Faint-pass pieces that are not streak-shaped would join a confirmed streak track under the rules of [ALGORITHM.md 13.4](ALGORITHM.md#134-anomalies-on-a-streaks-path). The first implementation leaves this out and discards them at extraction; reconsider after the first regression.
7. **Everything else comes from the normal pass only.** Point tracks, anomalies, slow movers, common motion, camera-fixed groups, residual analysis and photometry never see a faint-pass object.
8. **Provenance.** `DetectedObject` records that it came from the faint pass, so reports can show which streak tracks and single-frame streaks it found or extended. Its `peakSigma` and `integratedSigma` are in units of the smoothed frame's noise and are not comparable with normal detections.
9. **Configuration.** `enableFaintStreakSearch`, default true; profiles without the field get the default. There is no other new setting: the width follows the stars and the thresholds are the run's. With the switch off, results are byte-identical to today.
10. **Telemetry and progress.** A stage of its own, with its time and counts: candidates, vetoed, duplicates dropped, path pieces joined, and faint detections in the final tracks.

**Agreed by the maintainer (2026-11-01):** everything in this proposal except the single-frame gate below, including the API additions (the new `DetectedObject` field and configuration field need a compatibility note and updated consumer checks). `inspectTransients` and `redetectFrame` stay normal-pass only.

**The single-frame gate for faint-only streaks.** **Decision (made, 2026-11-01):** "no peak gate". The maintainer had suggested a much lower gate than `singleStreakMinPeakSigma` for streaks that only the faint pass finds; the measurement below supported dropping it.

- A streak track of two or more pieces has no peak-sigma gate; `singleStreakMinPeakSigma` (default 7) applies only to a streak that links to nothing. The Integral trail was found that way. Its pieces measured 4.2–5.6σ peak on the smoothed frames (for example 147 px, elongation 7.6, 5.6σ), so none would have passed as a single-frame streak.
- Every extracted object already has a seed pixel above `detectionSigmaMultiplier` (3.5σ by default), so any gate at or below the seed level means no gate.
- In smoothed frames the noise is correlated, so random blobs are larger, and some are elongated; for a single-frame streak this gate is the only significance check. A long faint streak's evidence is along its length, which `integratedSigma` measures better than the peak; with smoothing, the noise correlation comes from a known kernel.
- Measured on 2026-11-01 with a scratch program built on the engine's own code: the normal pass for frame selection and the star mask, then smoothing at the proposed width, extraction, the faint star mask, the duplicate test, and streak linking with the single-frame gate at zero. It stops before fragment assembly and consolidation. It ran on 19 datasets (the reference datasets and Integral-spacecraft-challenge, 775 kept frames), once on the frames and once on the negated difference `2 × median − frame`, which holds no real streak.
- **Frames.** 9 single-frame streaks were found only by the faint pass, on 8 datasets, and all of them are real trails on their cutouts. Five are below the current 7σ gate: 3.7σ (Apophis), 3.8σ (klangwolke-1), 5.05σ (Integral, a piece of the known trail), 5.3σ (m81-reg, a piece of a faint trail in 19 pieces) and 6.7σ (m101-reg). The multi-piece faint tracks reviewed by cutout were real trails too.
- **Negated frames.** No detection came from noise. All 9 single and 14 multi-piece detections were real structures that are dark in one frame and turn bright when negated: dark lines (Apophis frame 4, whynot frame 2, ic-5070), registration edges (whynot, ic-5070) and a frame border (Eagle-noisy).
- **Conclusion.** In smoothed frames, noise formed no streak-shaped object (elongation above 6 and at least 25 px) in 775 frames, so the shape rule, not the peak gate, rejects noise. No significance threshold would separate real from false here: the structural false detections spanned 3.2–153 integrated sigma (after the correction for the kernel's noise correlation), overlapping the real ones.
- **Recommendation.** No peak-sigma gate for a single-frame streak found only by the faint pass; it still needs the streak shape, the faint star mask and the binary-star check. No new setting. The full regression checks this end to end.
- **Smoothing width.** The extraction-FWHM rule gave σ = 0.67–1.41 px across the datasets. On Integral it gave 1.01 px, against 1.25 px from Siril's 2.9 px FWHM, because the extraction FWHM is cut at the grow threshold. At 1.25 px the trail's pieces in frames 4, 7 and 10 link at the streak-linking stage; at 1.01 px they remain pieces in frames 4, 5 and 7 (consolidation may join them later). The regression should compare the rule with a wider kernel.

**Acceptance.** Every reference dataset with the switch off (byte-identical to the base) and on. Per dataset, list every added, extended, merged or lost streak track and single-frame streak, reviewed with its pictures; no new false streak is accepted without the maintainer's review. On Integral-spacecraft-challenge, the trail is found as a streak track over at least the frames of the experiment (4, 5 and 7–10). Run time and peak memory are measured, klangwolke-2 included.

**Implementation status (2026-11-01).** Implemented as agreed, including the review responses, and described in [ALGORITHM.md](ALGORITHM.md), [CONFIG.md](CONFIG.md) and [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md). Results on the 19 reference datasets:

- With the switch off, every report is byte-identical to the base, apart from the allowed volatile fields.
- With it on, every normal streak keeps its track. New or longer real trails were found on 8 of the 19 datasets; each was reviewed with its pictures. Photometry is unchanged on the three datasets checked.
- Run time: about a third more detection time on the largest (61-megapixel) frames; the stage takes about 33 s on klangwolke-2. That run fitted in the 10 GB heap of the regression runs. The stage limits itself to two workers there and starts at 8.2 GB of heap in use; its own peak memory was not measured separately.

**Integral-spacecraft-challenge.** The trail is found as a streak track over frames 4, 5, 7 and 10, not over 4, 5 and 7–10 as this acceptance asked. The search smooths with the star-size width, 1.01 px here. The 1.25 px experiment reached frames 8 and 9; the 1.0 px experiment found frames 4–7. In the injection study, wider smoothing gave no consistent gain in recovery, so the star-size width was kept. **Decision (made, 2026-11-01):** "frames 4, 5, 7 and 10 are fine".

### ST-6 detailed review (2026-11-01)

**Assessment.** The streak-only second pass is a reasonable response to the failed whole-pipeline smoothing trial, and reusing normal quality selection avoids that experiment's changed frame rejection. The agreed scope and decision not to pursue oriented line filters should remain. This review includes the later 19-dataset, 775-kept-frame gate study added while the review was in progress. That is stronger evidence than the initial whole-pipeline trial, but the stated stop before fragment assembly/consolidation and the structural detections in the negated controls leave the issues below unresolved. P1 findings should be resolved before implementation; P2 findings need explicit specification and acceptance evidence. Source was traced and small numerical counterexamples checked; the real-data trials/cutouts were not independently rerun for this review. Application obligations are reviewed under [F17](../SpacePixels/FEATURE-REQUESTS.md#f17-detailed-review-2026-11-01).

1. **P1 — Guard the smoothing width against the existing “no measurement” sentinel.** [FrameQualityAnalyzer](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/quality/FrameQualityAnalyzer.java) returns `config.errorFallbackValue` when no usable stellar FWHM was measured. Its default is **999**, not a physical star width; the metrics also expose `fwhmStarCount`. Applying step 2 literally gives a Gaussian sigma of about `999 / sqrt(2) / 2.355 = 300` pixels for a sentinel-dominated session. That could erase streak morphology and make convolution prohibitively expensive. Derive the session width only from finite, positive, genuinely measured widths with valid supporting counts. Define what happens when none are available, bound kernel support, and record the effective width/fallback in telemetry. The approximate square-root-of-two conversion describes round Gaussian second moments; threshold-truncated footprints, blends and spatially varying PSFs still need validation. This does not require changing the binding EX-3 FWHM convention.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. The width comes only from kept frames whose `medianFWHM` is finite, positive, below `errorFallbackValue` and backed by `fwhmStarCount` of at least 5; their median is used. With none, the pass uses a fixed σ of 1.0 px and says so in telemetry. σ is bounded to 0.5–3 px and the kernel radius is ceil(3σ). Telemetry records the effective width, its source and the number of frames behind it. EX-3 is unchanged; the √2 conversion is validated by the injections of finding 9.

2. **P1 — Do not send faint candidates through the existing general transient population.** [TrackLinker.filterTransients](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/core/TrackLinker.java) places every surviving mobile streak into `allTransients`, including unlinked or single-gate-rejected streaks. [TrackingExports.buildPhase5AnomalyCandidates](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/core/TrackingExports.java) then offers unclaimed entries to [AnomalyRescuer](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/core/AnomalyRescuer.java), which accepts sufficient peak or integrated signal without a “normal origin only” condition. Simply adding faint detections to `validMovingStreaks` can therefore export faint-only anomalies and leftovers, contrary to steps 6–7. Inserting faint point pieces into `pointTransients` is worse: it reopens the point-linking/camera-fixed/residual path that the experiment showed was unsuitable. Keep separate normal, faint-streak and faint-path-piece collections, with explicit routing and ownership; reject/discard unused faint objects before building general anomaly/unclassified exports. A provenance field alone does not enforce this boundary.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. Faint candidates live in a collection of their own. They enter only streak linking and consolidation; a faint object that ends in no streak track is dropped before `allTransients`, anomaly candidates and unclassified exports are built, and none ever enters `pointTransients`. A test checks that a faint leftover never reaches anomaly rescue, residual analysis, common motion or camera-fixed rejection.

3. **P1 — Resolve the downstream-photometry promise rather than promising impossible isolation.** [JTransientEngine](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/engine/JTransientEngine.java) passes `trackResult.tracks` to photometry. [VariableStarAnalyzer.flagTrack](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/photometry/VariableStarAnalyzer.java) flags measurements along every track, including interpolated gaps. A correctly added faint streak can therefore change crossing flags, reference availability, the ensemble solution and variable-star verdicts, even if all photometric pixels and normal detections remain untouched. Normal anomalies intentionally absorbed into new streaks also disappear from their old category. Decide whether final enhanced streaks should affect contamination screening, and state that accepted effect explicitly. If strict normal-only photometric inputs are instead required, a separate track population must be passed deliberately. Validate all changed flags/ensembles and ownership, not only raw source lists; these effects cannot be ruled out by leaving extraction unchanged.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree; this is an accepted effect to state, not something to isolate. Final streak tracks, faint ones included, go to photometry's crossing flags as today: a trail across a star contaminates its measurement whichever pass found it, so flagging it is intended. Normal anomalies on a faint streak's path leave the anomaly category, as on any streak. The regression compares photometric flags, ensemble results and verdicts with the search on and off, and reports every difference.

4. **P1 — “Two pieces” is not “multiple independent frames”, and it currently bypasses the single-streak gate.** [StreakLinker.link](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/core/StreakLinker.java) allows same-frame links and accepts a chain when `points.size() > 1`, without applying `singleStreakMinPeakSigma` to that chain. SpacePixels' [TrackFindingIndex](../SpacePixels/src/main/java/eu/startales/spacepixels/report/TrackFindingIndex.java), however, classifies it as a single-frame streak when all points have the same frame ID. The new study's “multi-piece” category must therefore also be split by distinct observed frames; it is not necessarily independent temporal confirmation. Specify the policy for normal-only, faint-only and mixed-origin same-frame events, including how any retained normal gate interacts with a zero faint-only gate. Also specify whether the faint-only binary-shape screen applies before linking to all faint candidates or only to isolated singles, and how `enableBinaryStarLikeStreakShapeVeto` controls it. Its placement differs from normal linking. This finding is not a request to reinstate ST-4's declined global common-line/distance gate.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. Proposed policy: a streak track of two or more pieces needs no peak gate whatever their origin (today's rule for normal chains, unchanged). An isolated single needs `singleStreakMinPeakSigma` when normal and no peak gate when faint-only (decided). The binary-star shape screen applies where it applies today, to isolated singles under `enableBinaryStarLikeStreakShapeVeto`, for faint singles as for normal ones. Counts and the regression are reported by distinct frames, same-frame chains separately, as `TrackFindingIndex` classifies them. ST-4 is untouched.

5. **P1 — Existing `integratedSigma` is not a calibrated significance after smoothing.** [SourceExtractor](src/main/java/io/github/ppissias/jtransient/core/SourceExtractor.java) computes flux divided by `backgroundSigma * sqrt(pixelCount)`, assuming independent pixels. For a fixed aperture `A` after convolution, the variance is a sum over pairs of pixel covariances; measuring the smoothed pixel sigma does not supply those off-diagonal terms. As an illustration only, independent white noise convolved with a normalised 2-D Gaussian of sigma 1.25 has `sum(K^2)` about 0.05093: on an aperture large relative to the kernel, treating filtered pixels as independent can overstate integrated SNR by about `1/sqrt(sum(K^2)) = 4.43`. This is **not** a correction to apply blindly to adaptive blobs or already resampled real images. The new study says its integrated scores were kernel-corrected; document that estimator and distinguish it from the unchanged public `integratedSigma` field. Registration/interpolation correlations, masked boundaries and data-selected footprints still need empirical null/blank-aperture validation. Respect XC-1: no automatic numerical correction to the existing field without its required evidence and compatibility decision. The observed overlap between real and structural-artifact scores is good reason not to claim that a significance threshold alone solves the artifact problem. A zero faint-only gate is a candidate policy to decide and validate, not a reason to repurpose the normal single-streak threshold or silently add another setting.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. The public `integratedSigma` keeps its definition (XC-1). Faint objects' sigma fields are documented and labelled as measured on the smoothed frame, and no gate uses them. The scratch study's "corrected" figure was `integratedSigma × sqrt(sum K²)` = `integratedSigma / (sqrt(4π) σ)` for a footprint large compared with the kernel; it was a diagnostic only and does not enter the engine. No new setting.

6. **P1 — Specify duplicate association and canonical observation ownership.** “Overlaps” is not a duplicate criterion: convolution expands footprints, so a faint component can touch a different crossing streak or contain a normal bright fragment plus a newly exposed tail. Dropping every intersecting candidate can lose useful support; keeping it naively can count the same physical observation twice. The current rule also says nothing about a faint streak or path piece coincident with a normal point/anomaly. Existing assignment/export logic uses object identity; two detector passes create different objects even at the same pixels. Define stable, deterministic association using the relevant shape/location evidence, normal-measurement preference, ambiguous one-to-many cases and exclusive ownership. Cover crossing trails, close parallels, split/merged blobs, bright segments in faint tails and normal point tracks at the same location. Preserve original frame identity, filename, timestamp and exposure on every faint detection, especially after quality rejection creates gaps; those fields are stamped explicitly during normal extraction.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. Proposed association: a faint candidate duplicates a normal streak detection of the same frame when their footprints share pixels and their angles agree within `angleToleranceDegrees`. The normal detection is kept and the faint candidate dropped, including when it also covers a further tail, so one observation has one owner. A faint candidate that crosses a normal streak at another angle is kept. Candidates are ordered by frame, then y, then x, for deterministic results. Normal point detections and anomalies at a faint streak's position follow the existing rules (anomalies on a streak's path join it); no new coupling. Every faint detection carries its kept frame's sequence index, file name, timestamp and exposure.

7. **P1 — The union can change or lose normal tracks; unchanged extraction is not additive-result preservation.** `StreakLinker` greedily claims detections, preferring earlier frame gaps and then nearer continuations. Adding candidates before linking can change which continuation wins, not merely append points to accepted tracks. Consolidation can then merge histories and absorb normal anomalies. Define whether normal tracks have priority or whether rebuilding on the union and reviewing changed tracks is the intended contract. Establish deterministic ordering/tie behaviour and compare physical membership, splitting/merging and distinct frames. A boolean “from faint pass” only proves contribution, not that a track was newly discovered or extended relative to baseline. Step 6's extra path-piece join must also state how it interacts with `enableFaintStreakFragments`, existing normal claimed points, normal anomalies already joined, and the temporal/no-extrapolation rules of ALGORITHM.md 13.4. Unmatched faint pieces must not escape into any other category.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. Proposed contract: **normal priority.** Normal streak linking runs first, exactly as today. Faint candidates are then linked among themselves and can only join existing tracks through the existing consolidation rules, or form new tracks. A normal streak detection is never moved out of the track it has without the search; tracks can only gain faint pieces, or merge with another track through faint pieces (consolidation merges, it never splits). I also recommend **deferring step 6** (non-streak faint path pieces) to a later change. It raises most of the interaction questions here (fragment assembly, 13.4's temporal rules, claimed points) and is not needed for the measured recovery; it can be evaluated after the first regression. Without it, faint objects that are not streak-shaped are discarded at extraction. Fragment assembly keeps using normal pieces only, and may now attach them to tracks that contain faint pieces.

8. **P2 — Normalised convolution does not make boundary noise uniform or solve representation choices.** Near registration fill, the available kernel weights are renormalised differently, so `sum(K_normalised^2)` and the output noise vary with support. The ordinary data-area margin is three pixels; a Gaussian kernel can reach farther than that. Define finite kernel radius, valid-support threshold/area, treatment of interior legitimate dark pixels and how the local support affects thresholding or faint-only exclusions. Do not smooth fill into the sky or change the normal pipeline's data area as a side effect. Specify float/double working precision, short conversion/rounding if needed, saturation and low-noise quantisation tests. A Gaussian applied to narrow binaries or nebular filaments also changes morphology; the current binary-star shape screen needs new controlled tests, not an assumption that its old operating characteristics carry over.

   **Response (2026-11-01):** Agree. The kernel radius is ceil(3σ). Registration fill is excluded by the normalised convolution and stays fill. Faint candidates are kept only inside the data area shrunk by the kernel radius, so every kept candidate has full kernel support and uniform noise; the normal data area is unchanged. Working precision is float per row band, rounded to the stored 16-bit domain and clamped above the fill level and below saturation. A low-noise quantisation test is added; if rounding is shown to matter, the smoothed frame is scaled about the sky before rounding and the flux scaled back. The binary-star screen gets controlled tests on smoothed close pairs.

9. **P1 — The updated measurements leave a recovery mismatch and an incomplete false-alarm inference.** The original trial used `median(G * frames)` with smoothed-frame quality selection; the proposed design uses `G * median(frames)` and normal selection. Those medians need not match. The newer scratch study usefully follows the proposed order, but stops before fragment assembly/consolidation. More importantly, it reports Integral's proposed width as 1.01 px, with pieces in frames 4, 5 and 7, whereas the 1.25 px trial and acceptance require broader temporal recovery. Validate the full algorithm's required frame membership; consolidation cannot manufacture observations that no input path supplies. Do not assume that the earlier recovery transfers to the smaller kernel. The statement that `2 * median - frame` contains no real streak also needs qualification: median contamination/persistent structures can remain positive, and its noise includes median covariance. The study itself found nine single and fourteen multi-piece structural detections in those controls. Classifying them as non-noise is informative, but does not make the operational false-detection burden zero or establish that the same shape rule rejects bright instrumental structures in ordinary frames.
